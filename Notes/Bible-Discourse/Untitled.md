# Son of Man Coming on the Clouds (JESUS THE CLOUD RIDER)

## Summary
### Flow Summary
Second Maranatha Global Bible Study session on Daniel 7:9-14, presenting the Son of Man coming on the clouds as the divine Messiah and the center of the book of Daniel.

### Series Context and Structure of Daniel
- Study covers Daniel 7 to 12; Speaker 1 teaches the first 4 sessions, then alternates with Dalton Thomas.
    - Classes available on the Frontier Lines International app; sessions kept to about 45 minutes
- Daniel is written as a chiasm: chapter 7 is the peak, and verses 9 to 14 are a peak on top of the peak.
- Recommended reading: Samuel Whitfield's two-volume 'Son of Man, the Gospel of Daniel 7'.

### The Heavenly Throne Room (Daniel 7:9-10)
- Scene depicts God's courtroom and divine council at the height of the heavens, citing Michael Heiser's Unseen Realm.
    - Supporting texts: Psalm 82:1, Psalm 89:5-7, 1 Kings 22:19-23, Daniel 4:13-14 (watchers)
- The Ancient of Days is described in human form, unique title used only here.
- His throne is a fiery chariot throne (Merkabah) with wheels, paralleled in Ezekiel 1 and the ofanim in Jewish prayer.

### Judgment of the Beast (Daniel 7:11-12)
- The little horn (Antichrist) and the beast (empire) are treated as one and the same, a telescoping prophetic pattern.
- Lake of fire linked to the Valley of Hinnom (Gehenna) south of the Temple Mount; conscious punishment affirmed, duration uncertain.

### Son of Man on the Clouds (Daniel 7:13-14)
- Riding the clouds marks divinity (Deuteronomy 33:26, Psalm 68:4), so the Son of Man is God Almighty in human form.
- Daniel 7 merges promises of a human Messiah with promises of God coming from heaven, revealing complexity within God's oneness.
- Son of Man appears 78 times in the gospels vs Christ 49, Son of God 25, Son of David 14.
    - Aramaic 'Bar Anasha' points to Daniel 7, unlike Ezekiel's Hebrew 'Ben Adam'
    - Led to blasphemy charges against Jesus (Matthew 26) and Stephen's stoning (Acts 7)

### Daniel 7 Leopard Interpretation
- Speaker 1 lacks a clear interpretation of the leopard's four heads; fitting them to Alexander's successors feels like shoehorning.
    - Four wings as speed of conquest fits history; urges humility, fasting and prayer for insight

### Next Steps
- (Speaker 1) Revisit the Son of Man theme in the next class before covering the rest of Daniel 7
- (Speaker 1) Review histories of the post-Alexander period before teaching Daniel 8
